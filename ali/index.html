Resume

I began programming on the Amiga 500 and studied computer hardware engineering before working in film production, editing, and visual effects. That combination of technology and visual storytelling led me to advanced computer graphics, followed by an MSc and PhD in computer graphics and visualization. Since then, I have developed research prototypes, real-time 3D applications, mixed-reality experiences, and interactive systems across academic and industry settings.
