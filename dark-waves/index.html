01 A story in three acts

Different lives.
One story.
No single truth.

A young woman arrives from the sea with no memory. The isolated family that takes her in knows more about her than it admits.

Dark Waves follows the same mystery through multiple playable characters, each revealing a different side of the story.

Its narrative begins with an original screenplay by accomplished screenwriter and playwright Naghmeh Samini, then opens into an interactive form the player can inhabit, question, and influence.

Who you control changes how you play. Each character approaches the world differently, while the challenges they face are shaped by their personality, fears, relationships, and understanding of the truth.

04 From screenplay to game

The player changes.
So does
the game.

Dark Waves has been in development since 2023. Chapter One is the playable foundation; later chapters remain in screenplay form as they are adapted. Each playable character changes the rhythm and priorities of play. Their personality shapes how they read situations, the choices available to them, and what they must confront. The project brings the visual language and dramatic intimacy of film into a form the player can inhabit. It sits where games and cinema meet: cinematic in its staging, interactive in the way the story is discovered.
